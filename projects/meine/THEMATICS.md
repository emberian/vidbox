# Meine: the arc, and how to edit it now

Read this before changing anything in the piece. It is ember's direction.

## The arc (ember)

1. **The rant establishes the framing.** Hyde's satire ("thou shalt give Israel missiles"): the
   opening, and rant 2 at ~1:24, the split happening inside it.
2. **The American side:** US politicians and preachers over the **US arsenal** (forges, JDAM lines,
   the MK-84 shipment, airlifts), transitioning into the **actual bombing and strikes**.
3. **As the Israelis speak, the actual slaughters** (Netanyahu, Gallant, Dichter, Weiss, Eiland,
   Herzog: bars ~108-150 and minute 5). Under their words: Israeli forces killing people,
   ground-level shootings of civilians, aid seekers, medics and journalists
   (footage/shootings.json), and strikes on people.
4. **The final minutes: the ground**, the people of Gaza, untreated, lingering (the plateau and
   outro, as now).

The names of the dead run under all of it (a layer just above the Meine video), and the credits
follow the song.

## Pacing follows the music video itself

The piece is cut to Meine's own picture as much as its music: the car past the factories in the
daylight build, the car at night from ~4:15. ember: "we actually ALREADY DO AN **AMAZING** JOB OF
THIS". Changes keep that correspondence (industry with the arsenal, night with the killing) and
the pacing it already has.

## The working mode has changed

ember: "this editing we're moving into is a bit of a different type of work than we did before,
since before we had plenty of space in the video and few constraints, and now the constraints
really are piled up ... we've gotten in cuts of most of the video that we're quite proud+pleased of."

So, from here:
- **Surgical, not wholesale.** Change the layers a note is about; don't rebuild sections. Re-read
  the current section code and its NOTES before touching it.
- **Protect what works.** If a change touches a stretch ember or the visual review called strong,
  say so before making it.
- **Every rule still holds:** render/drc.py (0 errors), the untreated-once and
  nothing-over-the-dead gates, scope, hits on the grid, speakers named, subtitles for non-English.
- **Verify by looking**, with tools/contact.py 3x3 sheets of exactly the changed range, before
  reporting.
- **Be economical:** plan from the timeline JSON and existing frames; render only the changed range.
