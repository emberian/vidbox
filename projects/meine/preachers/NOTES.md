## How they could work in the first half (0:37–3:45, his second rant ~1:24)

1. **Seed the chorus before his rant (0:37–1:24).** One real head at a time, each landing a single word on the beat

   Hagee "**curse** those who curse you". Repeated Genesis 12:3 from five mouths turns the verse into a loop.
2. **Collision at the rant (~1:24).** Hyde: "thou shalt give Israel missiles". Then cut straight to Graham, "Give
   Israel the **bombs** they need", and Hagee, "we must give them the **weapons** of war". Then Hibbs, "insulting

3. **Call and response.** Hyde says "missiles"; the chorus answers "blessed / bombs / weapons / finish them / level
   the place". Vaughn's sung "what wonderful days" can sit under it as the hymn.

   all". Then Munther Isaac, from Bethlehem, alone and not multiplied: "the Bible is weaponized against us." He is

## Anti-war content beyond the preachers (per ember's note)

The heads say *bless, bombs, weapons*. The anti-war argument comes from showing **what the blessing buys**. Ideas
(each needs sourcing before use):
- **The receipts:** on-screen figures for the April 2024 Israel supplemental, House and Senate votes on C-SPAN, and
  DSCA arms-sale notices to Israel (Hellfire, JDAM, 2,000-lb bombs). His "trillion bajillion" against the real number.
- **The afterlife of a missile:** documented US-made munition fragments in Gaza. For example, the GBU-39 fragments
  reported after the May 2024 Rafah tent strike (CNN and others analysed them). Pairing these with "bless" is the piece.
- **Counter-clergy chorus:** Munther Isaac, plus Christian and Jewish clergy arrested at Capitol ceasefire sit-ins
  (Jewish Voice for Peace, Rabbis for Ceasefire). Their faces could *refuse* to multiply.
- **Tucker-style questioning as a device:** the clips where interviewers ask "where is that?" or "what land?" undercut
  the scripture without commentary.
- **Name everyone.** Real speakers get names and dates. That keeps the piece out of the deep-fake register and
  separates documentary from Hyde's satire.
