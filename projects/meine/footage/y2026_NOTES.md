# y2026 footage: notes

**Yes, there is a decent amount of 2026 footage.** It covers every month from January to October.
- The catalog has 97 clips and 374 segments, with 63 checked hit points.
- About 57 clips are Gaza, 18 are the Iran war, 11 are weapons production or transfers, 8 are Congress or protest, and 3 are Lebanon.
- Graphic levels across segments: 0 = 147, 1 = 178, 2 = 12, 3 = 37. 79 segments show identifiable victims, wounded people or mourners.

## Gaps
- **No 2026 footage of US munitions physically arriving for Israel from the US side.** No DVIDS loading of Israel-bound cargo and no US port shots were found. The only transfer imagery is Israel MoD's own airlift and port releases (Mar 9, Mar 26, Apr 30), which are censored (blurred pallets). The State Department's 2026 approvals ($2.8bn / 40,000 bombs in Sep; ~$450m bombs and bulldozers in Apr) exist only as talking-head news coverage.
- **Boeing**: nothing useful on JDAM or bomb production in 2026. The 2026 Boeing uploads found were air shows.
- **Hospitals inside Gaza**: arrivals and morgues are covered (Jul and Aug, AP 360p). There is no 2026 ward or surgery footage from WHO or MSF on YouTube; WHO's 2026 uploads were medevac talking heads.
- **UN / OCHA / UNRWA**: their 2026 YouTube output is mostly appeals and talking heads. Not catalogued.
- **B'Tselem / Forensic Architecture / HRW 2026**: mostly West Bank or report launches. Not taken because the lane is Gaza and weapons. **The West Bank in 2026 (settler attacks, olive harvest, demolitions) is very well covered** by AP, Reuters, C4, BBC and MEE, and would make a good separate lane.
- **Lebanon 2026** has a lot more available: AP Beirut strikes in Mar, Apr and Jun; IDF house demolitions; the Tyre strikes. Only 3 clips were taken.
- Hezbollah or Al Manar footage was deliberately excluded.
- Many AP Archive items are **360p only** (the source ceiling). Use them small, in a texture or composite role, or grade them heavily.

## Cautions
- **guardian-deconstructed-zinati-strike-2026-09-15** 2-10 shows the moment a 15-year-old is killed (graphic 3, identifiable). The rest of that clip is Guardian forensic annotation.
- Graphic 3 clips:
  - **mee-drone-tent-strike-bodies-hospital-2026-02-15**: blurred but bloody bodies.
  - **ap-khanyounis-morgue-bodies-2026-07-29**
  - **ap-jabaliya-strike-shifa-arrivals-2026-07-14**
  - the mass-funeral clips
  - **ap-father-sieves-bones-2026-01-24**: bone fragments.
- Identifiable children:
  - the crying boy in the Iftar crush, 16-20
  - the toddler and the boy with a wheelchair in the floods
  - the child photos on the remains in the MEE 112 clip
- **IDF releases** (strike-cam, spokesman videos, "Eliminated" cards, CGI targeting) are propaganda by design. They are useful as the voice of the machine, but their captions make claims (e.g. "weapons sites") that are not independently verified.
- **Corporate promos** (Lockheed, RTX) carry burned-in slogans such as "Delivering deterrence. Defending freedom." This is good for irony, but the captions sit over most shots.
- Strike-cam reposts carry broadcaster bugs and lower thirds:
  - Forbes lower thirds throughout
  - Roya watermarks mid-frame
  - APT logos
- Clean originals would be the CENTCOM and IDF releases on X or DVIDS, which were not pulled here.
- The Iran-war clips show damage on **both** sides: the Israeli homes in Dimona and Arad, and the Tel Aviv CCTV. They are tagged so that the piece doesn't conflate them with Gaza.
