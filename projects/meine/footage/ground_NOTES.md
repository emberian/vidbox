# Ground lane (Gaza): notes

148 clips, 585 segments, 54 frame-accurate hit points, 1.7 GB in `media/footage/ground/raw/`.
Contact sheets are in `media/footage/ground/sheets/` (`youtube_<ytid>_sN.jpg`, timestamps burned in). Hit strips are in `sheets/hits/`.
I looked at a sheet for every catalogued clip. Long clips (over about 4 min) are only reviewed in detail for their first 2 minutes, and their notes say so.

## Top 15 segments

1. **ap-drone-coast-road-return-2025-01-long** 0-34 and 92-111. A misty dawn river of thousands walking north between the sea and the dunes, then a top-down of walkers. Clean, muted and long, it's a natural bed for the head copies. Its walking cadence is about 0.55 s per step, roughly a 16th-note grid at 126 BPM.
2. **ap-zikim-flour-multitude-2025-07-30** 0-55. A telephoto ocean of people carrying white flour sacks through dust. It is the single strongest hunger image and is static, so it composites easily.
3. **afp-rafah-night-strikes-flares-2023-12** 0-25. Pitch black, then three orange domes of light (hits at 8.68, 11.56, 13.68). Perfect for an additive or screen blend over the faces, and for landing drops.
4. **ap-night-blasts-from-israel-2025-09** 0-10 and 37-46. The whole sky goes white-orange in a single frame (3.60, 24.84, 38.80/39.56). The biggest flashes in the set.
5. **nbc-night-tower-collapse-2023-10-08** 0-8. The frame goes from black to orange in one frame at 2.17. Maximum-impact hit.
6. **reuters-national-bank-strike-2023-10** 0-4.5 plus **ap-bank-strike-fireball-2023-10-08**. The same 8 Oct 2023 strike from two angles: a fireball at 0.50 (Reuters) and the AP rooftop fireball, then the smoke whiteout (22-29, usable as a wipe).
7. **wsj-residents-film-tower-strike-2023-10**. A balcony phone video: a small flash at 1.84, the filmer flees inside, then comes back to a black cloud swallowing the tower. Shows the human scale of fear.
8. **newscomau-gaza-city-towers-2025-09** 52-72. The blast wave hits the filmer's window at 63.56 and glass and debris fly. Also 108-113, a night white flash at 109.76.
9. **guardian-ansar-tent-strike-2026-03** / **ap-ansar-tent-strike-fire-2026-03**. A fireball over a tent camp at night (1.60 / 2.88), then the camp burns (AP 31-100, keyable fire). Pair it with **ap-gazacity-tent-camp-struck-2026-03-12** for the morning after, including the toddler on a red tricycle.
10. **ap-jabaliya-dig-for-victims-2025-03** 19-44. Men tear at concrete and dig sand with bare hands, about 1.0 s per motion cycle. Rhythmic.
11. **nbc-nasser-courtyard-graves-2024-01** 16-53. Shrouded bodies lowered into sand graves in the hospital courtyard, and hands patting the sand down. Gold palette, devastating.
12. **ap-aqsa-shroud-laundry-2023-10** 80-115. A morgue worker measures and cuts white shroud cloth. Quiet, hypnotic and non-graphic, but it carries everything. Good for the 2:15-2:45 breakdown.
13. **ap-nasser-hospital-wrecked-2024-04** 37-43. An 'EMERGENCY' sign seen through a blasted hole. Iconic still.
14. **ap-drone-khanyounis-2025-01-long** 60-79. An orbit around a torn apartment block with pink bougainvillea flowering in the rubble.
15. **ap-dusk-smoke-from-israel-2023-10** 55-88. Golden dusk with smoke plumes behind silhouetted trees. Beautiful and terrible, for the slow build.

## Gaps

- **Motaz Azaiza and Plestia Alaqad.** Their war footage lives on Instagram, and YouTube search only returns interviews about them. I did not try Instagram (rate limits and logins). A direct `yt-dlp` on specific public Instagram post URLs might work if ember picks posts.
- **Wael al-Dahdouh.** Only interviews were found, and the AJ live material is buried under banners.
- **Clean Al Jazeera Arabic b-roll.** Not searched. AJE packages always carry banners.
- **Close, daylight, ground-level moments of impact from inside Gaza in 2024-2025.** These are rarer than early-war ones. Most 2024-26 strike footage is aftermath or filmed from southern Israel.
- **Bisan Owda.** Only 2 of her many AJ+ episodes were taken. They are fully subtitled, so they mostly work as small inserts or cropped.
- **One age-gated Reuters clip** is listed under "wanted, needs login" in SOURCES.

## Before using clips

- **Pre-2023 material is in the catalog, labelled.** About 21 clips carry tags for the 2009, 2012, 2014, 2019 and 2021 wars or the May 2023 round (tags such as `2014-war`; filter on tags ending in `-war`, plus `2019` and `2023-may-round`). Several of the strongest collapse shots are May 2021 (al-Jalaa, al-Shorouk). If the piece implies "this war", leave them out or label them on screen.
- **Identifiable victims (82 segments).** These include the faces of dead children (andzu75JMCA 7-13 and vUqZl8zSwGA 4-11, both carrying AP's own "dead child" warning), grieving mothers (nbc-rafah-tent-fire-2024-05, which also has picture-in-picture and banners), injured children (aje-shifa-inside, c4-gaza-city-flight-hospital, mee-rafah-abu-jazar-house), the photos of missing relatives (aje-search-rubble-three-years), and a crying boy (ap-beit-lahiya-flight). These people did not consent to an art piece. Consider blur, distance, brevity or abstraction.
- **Militant material.** None was collected. Note that kSsvHTTEL3Y (AP, 7 Oct 2023) contains outgoing rocket volleys after 31 s, and y8rjCZXugAU (2014) contains rocket trails at 69-80. Neither is in a catalogued segment.
- **Weapons-lane overlap.** fa-rafah-bomb-identification (79-86), ap-mawasi-crater-tents (22-35, munition fragments) and bisan-cant-recognize-rafah (196-222, an unexploded bomb) show munitions on the ground.
- **Burned-in text.** AJE, NBC, Sky and BBC packages carry lower-thirds and tickers, and `overlay_ok: false` marks segments that need cropping. AP items carry a small top-right "AP" bug through almost everything. It is easy to crop or cover, since 360p material gets scaled anyway.
- **Resolution.** Most AP Archive uploads are 640x360 at source. The 720p material is AP "Associated Press" channel uploads, Reuters, AFP, NBC, Guardian, AJE and Anadolu.
- **Hits.** Each `t` is the visual frame. Distant strikes filmed from Israel have their own sound arriving 1-20 s late; the notes say so where it matters (2b6t, dzqtf2, 0Bf1t). Edit cuts and whip-pans are not hits, and the notes call them out where the tool flagged them. Many smoke-column segments have no onset in frame (the clip starts after the strike), so their `hits` is `[]`.
- **motion_tempo** is set on 38 rhythmic segments: walking crowds at about 0.55 s per step, digging by hand at about 1.0-1.2 s, excavator scoops at about 3 s, ambulance light-bar strobe at about 0.5 s, and the shroud-cutting at about 1.4 s.

## Tooling (for my future self)

These are in the session scratchpad and are not committed. `sheet.py` makes contact sheets with timestamps, `frames.py` tiles every frame in a range with its pts (this is how the hits were confirmed), and `hitstrip.py` wraps `tools/hits.py` and stacks the strips. `cat.jsonl` and `patch.jsonl` were merged by `build.py` into ground.json. Future edits go straight into ground.json.
