# Shootings lane: notes for the edit

## The strongest segments

1. **itv-ramzi-abu-sahloul, 150–172 s.** Men walk with their hands up and a white flag; one drops. This is the clearest "shot in the street" image in the set, it has natural sound, and the fall hit is at 163.45 (checked on 0.12 s strips). The aftermath at 172–200 s has a second report at 183.64.
2. **cnn-hala-khreis, 9–22 s.** A grandmother with her grandson and a white flag. The shot is a single-frame camera jolt at 13.02, and she falls at 13.26. CNN narrates over it, so put it under the music. Crop to the zoomed inset on the right; the picture is low-res and comes from MEE.
3. **nyt-rafah-paramedics, 285–345 s.** The lights-on convoy, then gunfire at 325.19 (refined) and in bursts after it. The audio is the dead paramedic's own phone. The NYT near-black stretch with captioned audio (about 336–500 s) is a devastating sound bed. The end card at 878–898 s shows all 15 faces with their names.
4. **guardian-jenin-surrendered-men, 22–46 s.** A man surrenders, lies down, and soldiers shoot him. Shots at 29.85 and 34.22, natural sound.
5. **fa-shireen-abu-akleh, 150–175 s.** The first burst is at 157.45, then the cameraman runs. It has natural gunfire. The scope-view reconstruction at 330–370 s is a cold counter-image.
6. **reuters-issam-abdallah, 13–24 s.** The journalists' live camera blows out when the tank shell hits, at 16.9.
7. **aje-gaza-city-aid-gunfire, 0–7 s.** A crowd breaks and runs under gunfire, with hits at 1.01, 1.49 and 1.78.
8. **dw-jenin-jeep, 96–112 s.** A wounded man strapped across a jeep hood driven past ambulances. This is the "shoved / treated as an object" image. There are no hits; cut it to the movement.
9. **aje-qabatiya, 23–58 s.** Soldiers tipping bodies off a roof, the "shove" image ember asked for. The push frames did not resolve at this resolution, so place them by eye.

## Natural sound vs produced audio

- **Natural sound:** NYT Rafah (Radwan's phone), ITV Abu Sahloul, Guardian Jenin, FA Shireen (scene audio under the analysis), AJE Gaza City aid (under captions), Channel 4 GHF night clip, AP clips (no voice-over in the archive cuts).
- **Produced or narration, no usable natural sound:** The Independent PRCS clip (bed/voice-over; use the NYT for sound), CNN Hala Khreis (Ward's narration), CNN flour massacre (correspondent), news.com.au Issam Abdallah (narration over the scene audio), Earshot (silent graphics), DW and Sky (reporter voice-over).
- Watch for music beds under the dead: none of the clips I catalogued has a music bed under bodies, but check the Independent and news.com.au packages before using their audio.

## Caveats to keep on screen

- The CNN flour-massacre drone video is the IDF's own edited release. Label it as such.
- The AP GHF contractor footage shows **US contractors** firing, not the IDF. It is powerful for the US angle; label it exactly that way.
- Rouzan al-Najjar is 2018, not the current war.
- Israel calls the two men killed in Jenin wanted militants. What the footage shows is men killed after they had surrendered.
